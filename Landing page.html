Hampi

The ruins of a medieval capital

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire in the 14th to 16th centuries. Its temples, markets and royal enclosures now sprawl across boulder-strewn hills in Karnataka, and it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Vitthala Temple

Known for its stone chariot and musical pillars that produce different tones when struck. It is one of the most photographed monuments in the site.

Virupaksha Temple

An active temple dedicated to Shiva that has been in continuous worship for centuries. Its gopuram rises above the bazaar street.

Getting there

The nearest major town is Hosapete, about 13 km away. Local buses and autorickshaws run between the two, and the best time to visit is between October and February.
